Recovery Coach

Tell it where it hurts, how bad, and since when. It computes a recovery modality and pulls a ranked set of rehab exercises live from the wger exercise database.

Model Transparency — read before using
  • This tool applies a general RICE-protocol heuristic (Ice/Heat/Rest/Compression) and is not a diagnosis or a substitute for medical advice.
  • Exercise suggestions come from the wger open exercise database, a community-maintained dataset — muscle and equipment tagging can be incomplete or inconsistent for some exercises.
  • There is no per-exercise difficulty rating in the data, so "intensity" is estimated from your reported severity, not from a clinical assessment of the exercise itself.
  • Sharp or severe acute pain is flagged for rest and professional evaluation rather than given an exercise ranking — persistent or worsening pain should always be checked by a clinician.
